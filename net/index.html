Enemy territory (scan)
Load the territory_mask.json of a territory scan: every other alliance's ground tile by tile, so your banners can pass diagonally between enemy squares that only touch at a corner. Pieces with your alliance tag are left out; click one in the list (or pick "Scan piece" and click the map) to switch it on or off.
Tiles the scan never saw: the icons and names of HQs and outposts hide blocks of them, right where networks meet. "Enemy if they touch an enemy": a block of unseen tiles is enemy ground when it touches a tile of an enemy piece, else free. Inside your own HQ's and CSV banners' squares they always count as yours. The networks list the unseen blocks they rely on, to check in the game.
Enemies
Pick "Enemy banner" or "Enemy HQ" and click a tile: it adds one there, or removes the one already there. Their squares are another alliance's ground: no banner of ours on it, and it is never ours.
Search
Banners of the pasted CSV's network. Below 1 the networks keep them where they can; one variant in three starts from the current network. 1 = no preference.
The minimums are a floor, more is a bonus: at 0.5, two extra nodes are worth one banner. A banner is only taken away when that saves more than the nodes it holds are worth, and empty banners move next door when that completes a node. 0 = above the minimum nothing counts.
First, with a pasted network, step 0 fixes its cheap errors (a banner moved up to 2 tiles, or one new banner as a bridge) and the variants start from the fixed network. Each variant: about 4 s to build the network with the most resources, under 0.1 s to take away what isn't needed, then the search time, which keeps lowering the count. The variants run side by side on up to 4 cores (one left free).
